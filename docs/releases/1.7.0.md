The first Windows installer for **Storage Tree**: see what fills a disk, on this PC or on an Android phone connected by USB or Wi-Fi, and clear it out.

- Folder tree, treemap, largest files and file types, filling in while the scan runs.
- Scan a folder or a whole drive on this PC, including files Windows keeps locked and folders only an administrator may open.
- Scan an Android phone's shared storage, with well-known folders explained and the size of every app.
- Delete what you don't need: to the Recycle Bin on this PC, for good on a phone (the app says so before asking).
- Save files and folders from a phone to this PC. In 1.7.0 this is about 7 times faster on big files: a 4.6 GB video took 16 seconds over a fast USB connection.

Everything runs on your own PC.

## Known limits

- Apps' private data on a phone can't be read without rooting it, so a scan doesn't include it. App sizes come from Android instead.
- Scanning and saving have been tried on one phone (a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold6, over USB). Connecting over Wi-Fi, deleting on a phone and dragging files out to Explorer have not yet been tried on a real one, so check the first results.
